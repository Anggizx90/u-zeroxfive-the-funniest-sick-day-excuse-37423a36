# NOTES — how the line was chosen

Competition: The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse. Judge criteria, in order:
1. Half-believable reason to call in sick that gets the biggest laugh.
2. Fresh idea (not fever / dog / dead grandmother).
3. Twist or funniest word near the end; lands on one read.
4. Sounds like a real person typing a message.
5. No wasted words.

## Round 1 — 22 candidate lines (drafted fresh, none copied)

1. Can't come in today — my smart fridge locked me out of the kitchen and I've been negotiating with it since 6am.
2. Sorry, can't come in — I sneezed so hard my back went out and now I'm horizontal for the foreseeable future.
3. Calling in sick: my toddler drew on my face with permanent marker and HR would have questions.
4. Won't make it in, my washing machine flooded the flat and I'm bailing out the living room with a saucepan.
5. Can't come in — I got locked in my own bathroom and had to wait for my neighbour to break in with a butter knife.
6. Sorry, I have to call in sick, my cat sat on my laptop during a Teams call and somehow resigned for me.
7. Won't be in today — I tried to fix the sink, flooded the kitchen, and the plumber says I need adult supervision.
8. Can't come in, my alarm didn't go off because my phone fell in the toilet and I had to fish it out with tongs.
9. Calling in sick — I pulled a muscle reaching for the remote and my doctor says the couch is now my office.
10. Won't be in today, my neighbour's parrot learned my boss's voice and has been scheduling meetings for me.
11. Sorry, can't make it — I got stuck in the lift with a stranger for two hours and we've agreed to start a podcast.
12. Can't come in, my car got towed while I was still in it and the impound lot says I count as cargo.
13. Won't be in today — I swallowed a fly, panicked, and now I've convinced myself I need a day to recover.
14. Sorry, can't come in — my smartwatch says my stress level is "critical" and I believe it more than you.
15. Won't make it in, my houseplants staged a revolt and I spent the morning negotiating a peace treaty with a fern.
16. Can't come in — I got my head stuck in the stair railing trying to prove to my kid it couldn't happen.
17. Sorry, I can't come in today, my shower broke and I'm currently held hostage by a cold tap and a locked door.
18. Won't be in today — I ate a gas station burrito at 2am and my stomach has filed a formal complaint.
19. Can't come in, my GPS took me to a farm instead of the office and the goats won't let me leave.
20. Sorry, can't make it in — I sneezed during my morning stretch and now my neck only turns left.
21. Can't come in today — my dog learned to open the fridge and now we're both too scared to leave the house. (dog — rejected, worn)
22. Calling in sick: my dentist says I need to rest after my wisdom tooth extraction, and honestly so does my wallet.

Rejected outright: #21 (dog, worn), #22 (flat, no twist), #13 (thin), #14 (snarky, not a sick reason), #10 (parrot is a stretch, no reason to stay home).

## Round 2 — finalists, sharpened

- A. "Can't come in today — I got my head stuck in the stair railing proving to my kid it couldn't happen."
- B. "Sorry, can't make it in — I sneezed during my morning stretch and now my neck only turns left."
- C. "Can't come in — I tried to fix the sink, flooded the kitchen, and the plumber says I need adult supervision."
- D. "Calling in sick — I pulled a muscle reaching for the remote and my doctor says the couch is my office now."
- E. "Can't come in, my car got towed while I was still in it and the impound lot says I count as cargo."

## Round 3 — pairwise, criteria in order

- A vs B: B is the more believable "injury", but A gets the bigger laugh — the
  visual of a grown adult wedged in a railing, plus the hubris of doing it to
  prove a point to a child. Criterion 1 decides for A (bigger laugh wins).
- A vs C: both believable domestic disasters; C's flood is a familiar trope and
  its punchline ("adult supervision") is a known joke shape. A is fresher (criterion 2).
- A vs D: D's twist ("the couch is my office now") is cute but the setup is
  weak — a remote-reach injury reads as lazy, not funny. A wins on 1 and 2.
- A vs E: E is the funniest image of the five, but "I count as cargo" is
  hard to half-believe and the excuse doesn't explain staying home sick — it
  explains being towed. A wins on criterion 1 (still a reason to stay home).

## Winner: A

"Can't come in today — I got my head stuck in the stair railing proving to my kid it couldn't happen."

Why it wins: it is a real, sendable message (criterion 4 — "Can't come in
today" is how people actually text a boss); the premise is just plausible
enough (parents do stupid demonstrations for kids); the idea is fresh
(no fever, no dog, no dead grandmother); the twist lands at the very end
("proving to my kid it couldn't happen" reframes the whole accident as
self-inflicted show-offery); and every word earns its place — 100 characters,
no filler. The laugh is the humiliation, which is exactly what a coworker
would quote back all week.
